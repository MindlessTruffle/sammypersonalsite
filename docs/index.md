# Sammy Hawari

Source: https://sammyhawari.com/

By Sammy Hawari.

Sammy Hawari (mindlesstruffle) is a software developer in Waterloo, Ontario, building websites, creative tools, and games. Explore his projects and experience.

Sammy attends Laurel Heights Secondary School in Waterloo, Ontario. His portfolio lists a Data Science Intern role at Yupp and Founder & Lead Developer roles at Inspect Element and SLOPIFY LIVE.

## Projects

- [The Number Company](https://sammyhawari.com/number-company/): I sell regular t-shirts, but each time one is sold the number on the next shirt goes up!
- [Neo Developer League](https://sammyhawari.com/neodev/): A series of interactive web experiences for Neo Developer League, exploring how a hackathon website can feel like a place you move through.
- [Icon Emulator](https://sammyhawari.com/icon-emulator/): A local workspace for generating game UI icons, emblems, and interface assets, with a library that keeps the useful results organized.
- [Larpmegle](https://sammyhawari.com/larpmegle/): A three-person video game about convincing someone you know more than the other person. Two players argue their case; a judge decides the winner.
- [Backflip Off a Cliff](https://sammyhawari.com/backflip/): A cliff-diving game project built around the thrill of jumping from taller places, learning tricks, and finding your own style.
