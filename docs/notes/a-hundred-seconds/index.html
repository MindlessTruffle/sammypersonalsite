a hundred seconds

the format

Larpmegle puts three people in a room: two larpers and one judge. The challenge is to convince the judge you know more about a shared topic.

the rhythm

Twenty seconds for player one, twenty for player two, then one more turn each. The final twenty seconds belong to the judge. The entire round lasts 100 seconds.

the screen

The active speaker gets the largest camera tile. The other two participants stay visible, and the judge has a notepad for keeping track of the arguments.
