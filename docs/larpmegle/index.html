100 seconds

Players choose a category and join as a judge or a larper. Each larper gets two 20-second turns. The judge gets the final 20 seconds to explain their pick. Categories include music, gaming, science, movies, and television.

making the match work

The prototype includes public queues, private room codes, WebRTC camera connections, timed microphone turns, and ranked results. The active speaker gets the main camera tile, with layouts for desktop and phones.
